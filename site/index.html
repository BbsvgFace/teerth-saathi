1Context

Kumbh Mela

The Kumbh Mela is one of the world's largest recurring religious pilgrimages. Rooted in centuries of Hindu tradition, it brings together millions of devotees to participate in sacred bathing rituals, spiritual gatherings and religious processions.

Ritual bathing is central to the pilgrimage, while religious processions, spiritual gatherings, charity and community activities form part of its wider cultural significance. UNESCO recognized Kumbh Mela as Intangible Cultural Heritage in 2017.

Photo: Rahul P / Pexels

What is happening?

The Kumbh Mela takes place across four locations: Prayagraj, Haridwar, Nashik and Ujjain. Its timing follows religious and astronomical traditions, with different sites hosting gatherings at different intervals.

An entire temporary city must operate across riverbanks and floodplains, supported by transportation, healthcare, sanitation, security, emergency services and thousands of people responsible for keeping it functioning.

As crowds move and conditions change, the ability to identify incidents, communicate urgency and coordinate a response becomes critical.

Who are we aiding?

Volunteers are among the most dynamic and essential parts of the Kumbh Mela’s operations. They take on a wide range of responsibilities, from assisting pilgrims and managing crowds to responding to emergencies, helping ensure the smooth functioning of the gathering.

The Kumbh Mela is held across four principal locations—Prayagraj, Haridwar, Nashik and Ujjain—with the timing tied to religious and astronomical traditions.

Who reports to whom?

Pilgrims and volunteers occupy the ground level of the system. Volunteer captains coordinate teams and respond to local incidents. Supervisors and command-centre authorities oversee broader operational needs, while medical, security and disaster-response teams provide specialized support.

Volunteers

Studying the Constraints

Operational constraints

  • Low tech literacy among volunteers
  • Battery-efficient operation
  • Works in poor or no connectivity
  • Accessible for diverse users

Environmental constraints

  • Extremely high crowd density
  • Large geographic event space
  • Temporary infrastructure
  • Intermittent network connectivity

App accessibility for all people

Efficient battery usage

The people operating this might not be proficient with phone usage

Accounting for cascading failures

The system must function under conditions that make ordinary digital interactions difficult.
